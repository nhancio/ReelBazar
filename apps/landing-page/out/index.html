Why Rava?

The gap between wanting it and buying it is where sales die.

Find creators who actually fit

Stop cold-DMing accounts and hoping. Matching reads category, audience age and style so you brief creators whose followers already shop your lane.

Attribution down to the reel

About Us

Fashion is discovered in reels. It should be bought there too.

People find what they want to wear by scrolling. Then the trail goes cold — a bio link, a screenshot, a search that never happens. Rava closes that gap: creators tag the pieces in their reels, shoppers buy in the same tap, and brands finally see which content sold what.
